01 / Professional profile

A perspective shaped by medicine, knowledge and responsibility.

Yaroslav Voydenko is a medical student and writer interested in the development of healthcare, contemporary medicine and the relationship between technology and society.

His work explores the challenges facing healthcare systems, emerging medical technologies and the importance of informed public discussion.

Through writing and independent research, he aims to make complex topics more accessible and contribute to thoughtful conversations about the future of healthcare.
